Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding registrations, booths, schedules, and speakers.

How can I register for an exhibition?

You can click on any "Register" or "Join Event" button which will direct you to our Registration Portal. Select the "Visitor" tab, fill out your company role, selected city, and basic contact details to generate your entry pass.

How can I become an exhibitor?

To register as an exhibitor, toggle the "Exhibitor Registration" tab on our registration page. Enter your company profile, booth space requirements (e.g., size), and team sizes. Our support representatives will reach out within 24 hours to confirm floor coordinates.

How do I book a specific booth number?

Once registered and approved as an exhibitor, you can access your User Dashboard. Navigate to "Booth Booking" to view a real-time floor map showing available (Green), reserved (Yellow), and occupied (Red) stalls, and select your preferred coordinates.

Can I register as a visitor for free?

General entry visitor passes are free of charge if registered online before early-bird deadlines. VIP passes which include premium networking lunches and conference keynote seating require payment vouchers.

How can I become a speaker?

Please contact our team directly through the Contact page form, selecting "Speaker Proposal" as the subject. Attach your portfolio, summary of research topic, and previous panel recordings for review.

Where can I find detailed event schedules?

The "Events" page displays dates, room numbers, topics, and speaker details for all upcoming workshops, summits, panels, and product launch sessions.
