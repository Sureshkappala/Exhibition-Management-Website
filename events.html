Event FAQs

Frequently asked questions about tickets, parking, and event accessibility.

Do I need to print my badge/ticket?

No, digital tickets on mobile devices are fully accepted. You can scan the QR code at the E-Check-in kiosks to receive your printed badge.

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, multi-level underground parking is available with 2,500 spaces. Parking is free for badge holders, but spaces are on a first-come, first-served basis.
