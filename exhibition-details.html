About the Exhibition

TechInnovate Global Expo stands as the premier technology summit of the year. Bringing together software architects, electronics manufacturers, tech researchers, and innovators, the event offers a platform to showcase smart systems, automation, AI bots, and cybersecurity tools.

Across three dynamic days, visitors can explore interactive display booths, attend panel discussions led by industry experts, and network with key decision-makers during VIP roundtable sessions.

Event Highlights

  • 500+ International Exhibitors
  • 30+ Dedicated Speaker Sessions
  • Interactive VR & AI Demonstration Zones
  • One-on-One Business Matching Dinners
  • EV Concept Cars & Hydrogen Systems
  • Cybersecurity Hackathon Pitches
