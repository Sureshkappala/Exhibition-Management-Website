Exhibitor FAQ

Quick support answers regarding shipping and logistics services.

Is shipment storage available before the expo?

Yes, our official logistics partner provides free freight storage at the advanced warehouse up to 7 days before the event. Deliveries directly to the hall are only allowed during official setup days.

How can I order extra electricity or AV gear?

All additional power sockets, screen panels, spotlights, and lead-retrieval scanners must be ordered through the Exhibitor Portal in your Dashboard at least 14 days before setup.
